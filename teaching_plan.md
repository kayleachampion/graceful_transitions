Before Class:
* Students review a 'Reading Note' containing questions and instructions for preparing for class
* Students watch short lecture video from instructor
* Students read / watch materials to prepare for discussion
* Target: 2 hr prep (standard ratio of 2 hrs prep for 1 hr class time)

In Class:
* Cold calling from random generator for case / material discussion
* Socratic style discussion; students respond and instructor probes
* Student ideas immediately reflected on board (Miro)
* Transition to group activity
* Target: 1 hr discussion (approximately 10 minutes per question / board), 30 minute activity

After class:
* Discussion materials and recording posted to internal class site
